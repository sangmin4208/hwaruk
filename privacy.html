Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy describes how the HWARUK uploader app ("the App") handles data. The App is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the HWARUK YouTube channel (youtube.com/@hwaruk) to publish the channel's own music videos through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses the App

Only the channel owner signs in to the App. It is not offered to the public and does not request access to anyone else's Google account.

Data the App accesses

  • With the owner's consent, the App uses Google OAuth scopes for YouTube to upload videos and thumbnails, set video metadata (title, description, tags, privacy and scheduled publish time) and add videos to the channel's playlists.
  • The App reads the list of the channel's own playlists to find the playlist to add a video to.
  • The App does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and upload results (such as the uploaded video ID) are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than Google's APIs, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps. Revoking access stops the App from making any further requests; locally stored tokens can be deleted by the owner.
