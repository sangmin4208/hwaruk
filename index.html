Anthems that catch fire

HWARUK (화르륵) is the sound of something catching fire. We make arena indie rock with rap verses, stomping drums and hooks built to be shouted back — anthems for the ones who got knocked down and got up louder.

Every song is released as a lyric video on YouTube. Music is made with Suno AI; visuals are AI-generated stills with code-driven motion.

About the uploader app

HWARUK publishes its own videos with a small private tool that uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and add videos to playlists on the HWARUK channel. It is operated only by the channel owner and does not offer sign-in to other people. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
